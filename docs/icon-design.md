# 应用图标设计

当前工程名为 WXStorageCleanUp，沿用原「微空间」图标；下方保留当时实际使用的生成提示词。

概念：聊天气泡与收纳抽屉融合，象征将聊天附件收纳整齐；一颗亮点表示腾出的空间。深绿色延续应用界面的主色，薄荷色与暖白色强调亲和感。该标志为独立设计，不使用微信官方图标。

源图：`assets/AppIcon.png`。通过内置 image_gen 工具生成，保留透明背景。`scripts/make_icon.py` 只做安装所需的尺寸派生及格式转换，生成包含 16–1024 像素的 `.icns` 图标。

## 最终生成提示词

```text
+Use case: logo-brand. Asset: production macOS app icon for 微空间, a small native utility that organizes WeChat chat attachments and frees local disk space. Create one highly polished square app icon, no lettering. Creative concept: a chat speech bubble cleverly becomes a compact open storage drawer, conveying tidying conversations and breathing room. A single sculptural ivory speech-bubble form with a short unmistakable tail, layered with a mint drawer/front panel and a clean horizontal negative-space gap. One small luminous lime four-point sparkle signals recovered space. Center the bold symbol on a deep evergreen/teal softly beveled squircle backplate. Restrained premium macOS aesthetic, tactile satin ceramic with subtle depth, clean geometry, soft studio highlights, balanced generous negative space, instantly legible as a tiny Dock icon. Front-facing with only subtle isometric depth. Color palette matches the app: evergreen #176E5C, mint #BDE9D8, warm ivory #F8FAF5, a tiny lime accent. Square 1024x1024 composition; squircle backplate occupies approximately 82 percent of the canvas with equal outer margin. Genuine transparent alpha outside the squircle, including its rounded corners. No words, letters, numbers, faces, eyes, badges, existing WeChat logo, mockup scene, extra objects, watermark or checkerboard pattern. Deliver only the final single icon.
```
